Microcredential Sample

Welcome to the Character, Flourishing and the Moral Purposes of Education sample

This is a sample of a micro-credential that will help you understand how character is formed through education and how its moral purposes can be made more visible in everyday practice. It is designed for teachers and school leaders seeking to connect character education with disciplinary learning, professional judgement and their particular school contexts.

Video transcript

Hello, and welcome to Character, Flourishing and the Moral Purposes of Education.

As teachers and educational leaders, we influence the character of young people every day, often in ways we don't consciously recognise.

But what does it mean to educate for good character? What is education ultimately for? And how might the subjects we teach, the relationships we develop and the decisions we make contribute to human flourishing?

These are some of the questions we'll explore throughout this micro-credential.

Across four modules, we'll introduce philosophical perspectives on character and flourishing, examine educational policy and professional responsibilities, and consider how character is formed through disciplinary learning and everyday school practices.

Importantly, we'll encourage you to connect these ideas with your own professional experiences and school context.

The micro-credential is entirely self-paced, so you can work through the modules at times that suit you. There are approximately 75 hours of learning altogether, with around 18 hours allocated to each module.

We recommend following the modules in sequence, taking time to engage with the readings, videos and activities as you go. You can revisit the materials whenever you wish.

You'll also encounter interactive activities, including opportunities to consider educational dilemmas, explore different perspectives and share your thinking. I really encourage you to participate in these, as they're designed to help you connect philosophical ideas with the realities of educational practice.

Throughout the micro-credential, you'll develop a reflective journal using Microsoft Sway, and you'll draw on a selection of these reflections for your final assessment.

Above all, I hope this micro-credential offers you an opportunity to think more deeply about the moral significance of your work, and perhaps to see familiar aspects of teaching in new ways.

I hope you enjoy the micro-credential!
