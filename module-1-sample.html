Module 1 Sample

Sample module summary

This module examines the philosophical origins of flourishing and its relationship to character and virtue, the contributions of philosophers to our understanding of the moral purposes of education, and how these perspectives might inform character formation through disciplinary teaching and everyday professional practice within your own school context.

In the full version of Module 1, you will:

  1. Gain an understanding of philosophical accounts of flourishing, virtue and practical wisdom in relation to education.
  2. Critically compare philosophical understandings of character, flourishing and moral development.
  3. Analyse the moral dimensions of professional judgement, relationships and disciplinary teaching.
  4. Reflect on professional identity and collective culture in schools.

The full Module 1 will include:

Topic 1: Philosophical foundations of flourishing and character education; Aristotle, Nussbaum, Tan and Weil; contemporary character education frameworks and research findings.

Topic 2: Teachers as moral agents; practical wisdom and professional judgement.

Topic 3: Professional influence, identity, ethical responsibility, care ethics and collective professional culture.

Topic 4: Critical perspectives on character education, collegial dialogue and reflective synthesis.
