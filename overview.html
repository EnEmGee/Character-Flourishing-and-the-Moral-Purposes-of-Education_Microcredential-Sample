About this micro-credential

This is a sample taster of Character, Flourishing and the Moral Purposes of Education. It gives you a taste of the ideas, features and learning experiences you will encounter in the full micro-credential.

The full micro-credential explores how character is formed through education and how its moral purposes can be made more visible in everyday practice. Designed for teachers and school leaders, it connects character education with disciplinary learning, professional judgement and different school contexts.

Through philosophical perspectives, research, educational policy and opportunities for reflection, you will consider your own professional practice and explore ways to cultivate character through teaching and leadership.

The full micro-credential is organised into four modules and takes approximately 75 hours to complete.

Time commitment

This taster should take you around 10 minutes to complete. People learn at different rates, so take the time you need to explore the content and activities before moving on.

Your reflective journal

In the full micro-credential, you will be invited to reflect on ideas, research and professional practices throughout each module.

You will capture these reflections and connect your learning with your own teaching or leadership experience. Your reflections will also contribute to the final assessment in the full micro-credential.

In this taster, you may encounter examples of these reflective activities.
